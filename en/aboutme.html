Graduated in Communication & Multimedia Design at HAN University of Applied Sciences.

Why did I choose CMD? +

I've always been curious about why people do what they do. How do they make choices? Why does one person find something obvious while another finds it confusing? At CMD I discovered that I can combine those questions with creative design. UX research in particular appealed to me right away.

What gives me energy in a project? +

A new assignment and working together with others give me energy straight away. I love exchanging ideas and learning from different perspectives. My favourite part is the research phase: talking to the target audience, listening carefully and discovering what's really going on. For me, that's the foundation of a strong design.

What am I like as a colleague? +

Calm and positive, but I take my work seriously. I ask questions when I don't understand something and I like working with people from different backgrounds. And I believe you should be able to have fun at work too.

What is my unexpected talent? +

Being funny in multiple languages. That might sound small, but try making a joke at exactly the right moment in a language you learned later in life. I'm secretly quite proud of that.

What did moving to the Netherlands teach me? +

I came to the Netherlands from Syria in 2015. Starting over was a big change, but it showed me how adaptable I can be. I trust that I can handle new situations, even when I don't yet know how everything will turn out. And I better understand what it feels like to have to find your place somewhere.

What have I taken away from my internships? +

That asking questions may be even more important than having an answer right away. At Centraal Beheer and Antea Group I learned that you don't have to be afraid to try things, get feedback or make mistakes. That's exactly how you grow and how your work gets better.

Can I code as well? +

Yes, I have experience with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. My focus is on UX/UI design, but that technical knowledge helps me create designs that can actually be built. I have a better understanding of what developers need to take a design further.

What do I do when my laptop closes? +

Running, cooking or baking, going to the movies or a fun evening with friends. Sometimes active, sometimes just relaxing together. I like that variety.

Why UX/UI?

Curiosity became my direction.

Research first

I chose Communication & Multimedia Design because I want to understand why people make certain choices and how they experience their surroundings.

During my studies I noticed that research is what energises me most. Talking with users, asking follow-up questions and discovering patterns: to me that's the most interesting part of the design process. A beautiful interface matters, but first I want to know who I'm designing for and which problem I'm trying to solve.

I like to work from those insights towards a clear and usable design. My knowledge of front-end development helps me with that: I can take the technical side into account and collaborate more easily with developers, while my own focus stays on UX/UI.
