ABOUT ME.

In 2015 I came to the Netherlands from Syria and had to learn to read a completely new environment in a short time — the language, the habits, the unwritten rules.

That habit of looking closely before judging is something I use every day as a designer: first understand why people do what they do, then design. I most enjoy working where research, interaction design and code meet.
