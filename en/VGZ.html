Main CMD assignment · VGZ

An interactive coach for moving flexibly & staying strong

An exercise app for people aged 65+, redesigned to be accessible, personal and in line with what really motivates older adults.

  • Role: UX / UI Designer
  • Duration: 5 months
  • Team: 5 students
  • Tools: Figma, Miro

Introduction

During the main assignment of my Communication & Multimedia Design programme, I worked with four fellow students and the VGZ design team on renewing the Soepel & Sterk Coach app (VGZ is a Dutch health insurer). The app helps older adults stay healthier and active for longer. The app was already successful, but VGZ wanted to improve the experience: more accessible, more personal and better tailored to the needs of older adults.

Context & Challenge

The existing Soepel & Sterk Coach app needed to become more accessible and more personal.

  • Different needs Older adults have widely varying physical abilities and digital skills.
  • Accessibility The app must be simple and intuitive to use.
  • Design challenge Designing an experience that fits personal preferences and motivates people to move.

Objective

Encourage older adults to exercise regularly and stay healthy and independent for longer.

  • Accessible Make exercising easy and approachable.
  • Personal Match individual needs and goals.
  • Motivating Support older adults in staying active.

Target audience

Adults aged 65 and over, with different physical abilities and digital skills.

  • Independence Staying healthy and active in daily life.
  • Social contact Value contact with others and exercising together.
  • Barriers May experience physical limitations or hesitation towards technology.

Design question

How can we design digital interfaces and interactions that encourage people aged 65+ to move more consciously and reduce physical complaints?

IBM Loop Framework

For this project we used The Loop from the IBM Enterprise Design Thinking framework. This model combines structure with flexibility and works well for complex design questions.

In a continuous cycle of Observe, Reflect and Make we worked on understanding the current situation and shaping future possibilities.

The strength of this approach is that insights are translated again and again into new ideas and prototypes. Prototyping and testing played an important role in validating and improving ideas straight away.

Research

The research had two tracks: Information Architecture and interviews with the target audience. This let us map the existing structure of the app and gain direct insight into the experiences and needs of the target audience. All findings were eventually brought together in an affinity map, from which clear opportunity areas emerged.

Information Architecture

In the IA analysis we examined the structure, flows and terminology of the app and the VGZ website. This led to the following findings:

  • Inconsistent forms of address (formal u vs. informal jij).
  • Overlapping categories that cause confusion.
  • Dead-end navigation paths without logical next steps.
  • Important features were not in an intuitive place.

Interviews & testing with the target audience

We talked to older adults to understand their exercise habits and use of technology, and tested the existing app to uncover its strengths and weaknesses. The key insights were:

  • A strong need for simple and clear navigation.
  • More guidance and personal explanation wanted.
  • A clear view of progress is motivating.
  • Busy interfaces or long instructions are actually demotivating.

Business Goals

During the playback session we defined the most important business goals together with VGZ. These goals formed the starting points for the further design of the app.

01

Motivation & health

Keep moving

Motivate users to finish programmes and start new ones.

Relieve complaints

Offer effective programmes that help reduce complaints, without immediately needing to seek care.

02

Ease of use & insight

Accessible design

Develop a user-friendly interface that suits the target audience of 65 and over.

Measure effectiveness

Gain insight into how much users are actually being helped.

03

Regulations & safety

Comply with regulations

All content and features must comply with VGZ's applicable regulations.

Optimise the questionnaire

Collect relevant insights within the legal framework.

Limit risks

Avoid medical terminology and minimise legal risks.

Affinity Mapping & Key Insights

All results were clustered in an affinity map. This led to clear key insights and sharper business goals:

  • Short, achievable activities hold attention.
  • A personal tone and social motivation make the app more appealing.
  • An easy start is crucial so new users aren't put off.
  • Business goals: more consistent content, a stronger brand identity, a focus on measurable health effects.

Opportunity areas

Two important opportunity areas emerged from the affinity mapping. Older adults draw motivation from social contact and exercising together. They also see exercise as a way to stay healthy and prevent physical complaints. These insights formed the basis for the concept development.

Opportunity area 01

Motivation & moving together

Older adults are motivated by exercising together and by social contact. At the same time, seasons and bad weather can get in the way.

Key insight

Social motivation is important, but exercise activities also need to remain achievable when exercising outdoors is difficult.

What participants told us
"I like walking with my husband."
"Exercise helps me keep in touch with people."
"In winter I can't cycle outside."
"How much we move depends heavily on the season."
Opportunity area 02

Staying healthy & preventing complaints

Older adults see exercise as an important part of ageing healthily. It helps them reduce complaints and stay both physically and mentally fit.

Key insight

Staying healthy and preventing complaints are strong motivations to exercise. The app can build on this with achievable activities.

What participants told us
"Exercise is important to me because of my back problems."
"I exercise because it's proven to add years to my life."
"My motivation comes from myself — when I haven't walked for a day, I feel guilty."
"We exercise a lot to stay fit and healthy."

Concept development

Move Naturally Together

After brainstorming and voting together with the VGZ team, we chose the concept Move Naturally Together (Beweeg Natuurlijk Samen). This concept links daily routines to exercise activities and makes progress visible. This creates motivation and reduces health risks.

Core interactions

  • Linking personal goals to routines.
  • Visually tracking progress with measurable factors.
  • Sharing results with friends or family.
  • Planning and doing exercise moments together.

Result & Impact

The renewed Soepel & Sterk Coach app translates the research insights into a concrete design:

  • Motivating and social: older adults stay more active because personal goals and shared exercise moments are central.
  • User-friendly: the app offers clear navigation and an easy start.
  • Health-focused: short, achievable activities help prevent complaints and promote vitality.
  • Expandable and modular: new programmes or features can easily be added.
